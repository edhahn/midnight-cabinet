# Editorial rules

- Never ask players to identify a film director, including indirect made/helmed/debut wording.
- Actor, casting, voice and narration identity questions require a reviewed film release year of 1978 or later. 1977 and earlier are excluded.
- Older-film characters, story, setting, creatures, studios and literary sources remain eligible. Incidental performer references do not make a question a casting question.
- Fictional actresses in novels are story characters, not film-casting trivia.
- Keep one supporting source per record, an evidence locator for expansion records, paired local illustrations and clear question/answer association. Read source passages before marking verified.
- Review semantic duplicates in addition to normalized question matching.
