# Content review rules

Do not ask players to identify a film director. This includes indirect wording such as who made, helmed, remade, or made their feature debut with a film. Actor, character, story, creature, setting, author and history questions remain eligible. Incidental director credits in sources do not disqualify a card.

Review meaning as well as automated wording checks. Every new batch requires a manual semantic pass; the automated detector is only a backstop. Preserve distinct facts and check replacements against the entire collection. Read supporting source passages before marking facts verified.

The one-for-one live replacements and their evidence locators are recorded in director-replacements.json. Stable historical IDs are retained even when a replacement changes the film.
